Personal Details

José Protasio Rizal Mercado y Alonso Realonda was born on June 19, 1861, in Calamba, Laguna, Philippines. He is known as the national hero of the Philippines. He was a physician, writer, poet, painter, and reformist. He died on December 30, 1896, in Manila.

  • Wrote the novels Noli Me Tangere and El Filibusterismo.
  • Became an ophthalmologist and studied medicine in Madrid, Paris, and Heidelberg.
  • Spoke and wrote in many languages.
  • Founded La Liga Filipina to promote peaceful reform.
  • Inspired generations of Filipinos to love their country.
  1. 1861 – Born in Calamba, Laguna.
  2. 1887 – Noli Me Tangere is published.
  3. 1891 – El Filibusterismo is published.
  4. 1892 – Founds La Liga Filipina.
  5. 1896 – Executed at Bagumbayan.
